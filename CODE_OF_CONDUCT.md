# Code of conduct

Ansy exists so that the web works for everyone. The people building it treat
each other the same way.

- Be kind and direct. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Disabled people are the experts on their own experience. When someone tells
  you something is a barrier, believe them.
- No harassment, no slurs, no contempt, in issues, pull requests, chat or
  anywhere else this project meets.

If someone crosses that line, tell the maintainers through the contact form at
https://slikk.dev/#contact. We read every message, keep it private, and act on
it. Anyone who keeps crossing
the line is asked to leave.
